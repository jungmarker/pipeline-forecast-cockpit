Method definitions

CRM stage-weightedAmount × default stage probability (Discovery 10%, Demo 20%, Proposal 50%, Negotiation 75%) over all open deals. The usual CRM dashboard number; it ignores timing, missing amounts and deals not created yet.
CRM + run-rateThe same, plus the average bookings from deals created and won within a quarter (last 4 quarters).
Conversion modelEach open deal's probability of closing won this quarter, by type, segment, stage and deal age, learned from earlier quarters and shrunk toward the stage average when data is thin. Missing amounts use the median won ACV for that type and segment. Plus the run-rate. P10/P90 come from 4,000 simulations.
